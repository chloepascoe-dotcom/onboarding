# Smartling: Paid Media Proposal

## Overview
Recommended paid media strategy for Smartling to drive qualified traffic and brand awareness among enterprise buyers evaluating localization solutions.

---

## Recommended Monthly Budget Allocation: $50,000

| Platform | Budget | % of Spend | Objective |
|----------|--------|------------|-----------|
| Google Ads | $22,500 | 45% | Capture high-intent search demand |
| LinkedIn Ads | $17,500 | 35% | Target enterprise decision-makers |
| Meta Ads | $7,500 | 15% | Retargeting + content amplification |
| Microsoft Ads | $2,500 | 5% | Incremental search coverage |

---

## Google Ads Strategy ($22,500/mo)

**Campaign Structure:**
- **Brand Defense** (10%) - Protect branded searches
- **Non-Brand Search** (60%) - High-intent keywords
- **Competitor Conquest** (20%) - Capture competitor searches
- **Display Retargeting** (10%) - Re-engage site visitors

**Priority Keywords:**
- translation management software
- localization platform
- enterprise translation services
- [competitor] alternative
- website translation tool

---

## LinkedIn Ads Strategy ($17,500/mo)

**Campaign Structure:**
- **Prospecting** (70%) - Cold audience campaigns
- **Retargeting** (30%) - Website visitors + engaged users

**Targeting Approach:**
- Job Titles: VP/Director of Localization, Product, Marketing, International Operations
- Company Size: 500+ employees
- Industries: SaaS, E-commerce, Gaming, Financial Services
- Skills: Localization, Translation Management, Internationalization

**Ad Formats:**
- Single Image Ads (thought leadership)
- Document Ads (case studies, whitepapers)
- Conversation Ads (demo requests)

---

## Meta Ads Strategy ($7,500/mo)

**Campaign Structure:**
- **Retargeting Only** - Website visitors, LinkedIn engaged
- **Content Amplification** - Promote high-value content

**Rationale:** Meta is less effective for B2B prospecting but valuable for staying top-of-mind with known audiences at lower CPMs.

---

## KPIs & Measurement

Since conversion tracking is not yet available, we'll optimize toward:

| Metric | Target | Platform |
|--------|--------|----------|
| CTR | >1.5% | Google Search |
| CTR | >0.8% | LinkedIn |
| CPC | <$8 | Google Non-Brand |
| CPC | <$12 | LinkedIn |
| Qualified Traffic | 5,000+ sessions/mo | All |
| Landing Page Engagement | >60% scroll depth | All |

**Interim Success Signals:**
- Demo request form submissions (manual tracking)
- Time on site from paid traffic
- Pages per session
- Return visitor rate
