Account Improvement Plan

May 12, 2026

Issue 1: Conversion Tracking

What Happened

Demo tracking broke, resolution took weeks, and the fix was incomplete (Google only, not LinkedIn). This resulted in 1+ month of unreliable data during active spend.

Safeguard: Tracking Validation on Changes

  • Site or landing page changes: When new landing pages are added or tracking changes are made, Mary will verify conversion tracking across all platforms within 24 hours
  • Cross-platform validation: When fixing tracking on one platform, we will audit ALL platforms within 24 hours

Safeguard: Escalation Protocol

  • Tracking issues are now classified as Priority 1 with a 24-hour resolution target
  • If not resolved in 24 hours, Chloe is automatically looped in

Issue 2: Reactive vs. Proactive Management

What Happened

Optimization ideas have been coming from your team rather than ours. You need a partner that flags issues early and brings specific recommendations, not one that waits to be asked.

Safeguard: Proactive Insights Framework

  • Weekly "What We're Watching" section in every report with 2-3 specific observations and recommended actions
  • Monthly Creative Audit: Review of all active creative with performance tiers (scale / test / pause) and specific recommendations for new concepts
  • Optimization section in bi-weekly call: Dedicated time for Mary to walk through what's working, what's not, and what we want to test

Safeguard: Performance Thresholds

  • We will define benchmarks together (CTR, CPC, CPL by platform/campaign)
  • When any metric falls below threshold, you'll receive a same-week recommendation with our POV on cause and fix
  • No more waiting for you to notice. We flag it first

Issue 3: Copy Quality

What Happened

Copy has read as generic AI content. You're rewriting more than you should when you hand things to us. You need a team that understands your business well enough to write like part of it.

Safeguard: Copy Calibration Process

  • Brand Voice Doc: We will create a living document with your tone, terminology, phrases to use/avoid, and 3-5 "gold standard" examples of copy you love
  • Copy review checkpoint: Before any copy goes live, we compare against the voice doc and flag anything that feels off-brand
  • Feedback loop: When you do rewrite, send us the before/after so we can learn what missed the mark

Safeguard: Brand-Trained Copy Process

  • All copy will be drafted with your specific value props, customer pain points, and competitive differentiators front and center
  • Any AI used will be trained on your specific company. 100% of all content will have human review and changes as needed to match your brand voice
  • If copy ever feels generic, tell us. We will rewrite same-day, no questions asked

Task Management

Safeguard: Clear Task Assignment

  • Any tasks that need to be completed should be assigned to Mary in Asana or mentioned on Slack with details so she can prioritize and add to her to-do list
  • This ensures nothing falls through the cracks and all requests are tracked and prioritized appropriately
