Session Details

Year in Review 30 min

Honest look back at the past 12 months of partnership.

  • Top 3-5 wins with metrics and context
  • Honest assessment of misses or pivots
  • YoY performance trends
  • What we learned that informs next year

Strategic Alignment 30 min

Understand their business direction to ensure marketing supports it.

  • Their business goals for next 12 months
  • Any shifts in audience, product, or market focus
  • Budget trajectory and investment appetite
  • Internal changes (team, leadership, priorities)
  • What does "great" look like a year from now?

Market & Competitive Landscape 20 min

Share what we're seeing in their space.

  • What competitors are doing (ads, content, positioning)
  • Industry trends affecting tax lien space
  • New channels or tactics worth watching
  • Threats and opportunities we see

Single Grain Presents: Annual Ideas 45 min

We come prepared with 5-7 ideas for the next year.

Each Idea Includes:

  • The opportunity - what it is
  • Why we think it works for them
  • What it requires (budget, effort, timeline)
  • Projected impact range
  • Risks and how we'd mitigate

Mix of: expansion of what's working, new channels, efficiency plays, creative bets.

Collaborative Brainstorm 30 min

Open floor for ideas from both sides.

  • What ideas do they have?
  • What have they seen competitors do that intrigued them?
  • What internal initiatives could marketing support?
  • Wild ideas welcome - we'll filter later

Prioritization Exercise 25 min

Work together to decide what matters most.

  • All ideas on the table (ours + theirs)
  • Score together on: Impact, Effort, Strategic Fit
  • Stack rank into: Do Now, Do Next, Explore Later, Park

Build the Roadmap 20 min

Turn priorities into a timeline.

  • Map prioritized ideas to quarters
  • Identify dependencies and sequencing
  • Flag resource or budget decisions needed
  • Align on what's confirmed vs. tentative

Commitments & Next Steps 5 min

  • Clear action items with owners
  • Timeline for proposals on new initiatives
  • When to reconvene for check-in

Prep Checklist (2 Weeks Before)

Data & Analysis

  • Pull 12-month performance data
  • Build YoY comparison
  • Competitor audit (ads, landing pages)
  • Industry trend research

Ideas Development

  • Brainstorm internally: 10+ ideas
  • Narrow to 5-7 strongest
  • Build mini-business case for each
  • Create presentation slides

Logistics

  • Confirm attendees both sides
  • Send calendar hold with agenda
  • Arrange lunch delivery
  • Test tech setup

Session Materials

  • Assign note-taker
  • Prepare Miro/FigJam for brainstorm
  • Create prioritization template
  • Roadmap template ready
